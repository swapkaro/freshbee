FRESHBEE — PLAYABLE MVP

Unzip this archive, then double-click Freshbee.html to open it in Chrome, Edge or Safari on desktop. No install or local server is needed. Enable hardware acceleration for 3D.

Click TAKE THE FIELD to play against AI, or Practice first to learn the controls.

WASD/arrows: run or aim while carrying
E: quick pass
Q: next receiver
Space: hold/release throw
Shift: sprint
R: switch athlete
H: huck
F: fake
X: dive
C: camera
Esc: pause and help

Players cannot run with the disc. Catch in the attacking end zone to score. First to five wins.

This portable edition saves locally in your browser when local storage is available. It is not the online server-saved edition.

Known limits: one detailed athlete base shared across two uniform variants; approximate aerodynamics; distant skyline is a photographic backdrop; no audio or online matchmaking.
